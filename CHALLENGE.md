## Elevator pitch (30 sec)

Bwiza Workbench is an AI co-pilot for clinic front desks in Rwanda. Patients describe symptoms in English or Kinyarwanda; the system drafts urgency triage, specialty routing, and clinician-ready notes. Nurses review and sign off — human-in-the-loop, not auto-diagnosis.

## Demo script (2–3 min video)

1. **Intro (15s)** — Problem: long queues, inconsistent triage, language barriers at health centers.
2. **Patient intake (45s)** — Enter symptoms: *"My child has fever and cough for 2 days"*. Show chat + triage card (Medium · Pediatrics).
3. **Clinician review (45s)** — Switch role, review note draft, click **Mark reviewed**.
4. **Kinyarwanda (30s)** — Switch locale to RW, show bilingual UI.
5. **Close (15s)** — Built with React, Express, SQLite; extends Ubuzima Bwiza / MoH experience; IBM Bob for next iteration.
